True to colour

Daylight near a window, no direct sun. Lock focus and exposure by tap and hold. No colour filters or AI enhancers. Hold your phone next to the real cake: colours should match. Clean, light background.

Shot list

Must have: 1 The hero (whole dessert, glaze shining). 2 Close-up texture. 3 The cut: cut straight down, hold the cut side to camera for 2 to 3 seconds. 4 First bite and honest reaction.
Bonus: 5 Real fruit side by side. 6 Box or gift moment. 7 Name and location on screen.

Clean cut: long thin knife dipped in hot water and wiped dry, one smooth push down, cake cold from the chiller.

Video flow (a suggestion)

0 to 2 s hook. 2 to 10 s hero and close-up. 10 to 20 s the cut. 20 to 30 s first bite. Last 3 to 5 s box or name and location. Vertical 9:16, HD. Use your own style.

Handling and tone

Keep the dessert in the chiller until you are ready. Film within 10 to 15 minutes out of the chiller. Don't leave it in the car or sun.

Be warm, honest and real. Say "No alcohol, no pork, no lard". Please don't say "halal", don't use fake hype or fake urgency, and check prices and flavours with us first.
