Our desserts, in a few words

  • Modern French entremets and petit gâteaux, made fresh at our atelier in Larkin, Johor Bahru
  • Layered inside: mousse, insert, crunch and sponge. The cut shows it best
  • No alcohol, no pork, no lard
  • Order by walk-in, WhatsApp, or chaleurpatisserie.orderla.my

True to colour

  1. Daylight near a window, no direct sun
  2. Lock focus and exposure: tap and hold on the cake
  3. No colour filters or AI enhancers
  4. Hold your phone next to the real cake: colours should match
  5. Clean, light background

Shot list

Must have
  1. The hero: whole dessert, glaze shining
  2. Close-up texture
  3. The cut: cut straight down, hold the cut side to camera for 2 to 3 seconds
  4. First bite and honest reaction
Bonus
  1. Real fruit side by side
  2. Box or gift moment
  3. Name and location on screen
Clean cut
  1. Long thin knife, dipped in hot water and wiped dry
  2. One smooth push down
  3. Cake cold from the chiller

Video flow (a suggestion)

  1. 0 to 2 s: hook
  2. 2 to 10 s: hero and close-up
  3. 10 to 20 s: the cut
  4. 20 to 30 s: first bite
  5. Last 3 to 5 s: box, or name and location
Vertical 9:16, HD. Use your own style.

Handling

  1. Keep the dessert in the chiller until you are ready
  2. Film within 10 to 15 minutes out of the chiller
  3. Don't leave it in the car or in the sun

Tone: what to say

  1. Be warm, honest and real. Your own words are best
  2. Say "No alcohol, no pork, no lard". Please don't say "halal"
  3. No fake hype or fake urgency
  4. Check prices and flavours with us first
  5. Say it was a collab, for example "Collab with @chaleuratelier_my"

Caption must include

  1. Dessert name
  2. @chaleuratelier_my tag and location: Chaleur Atelier Pâtisserie, Larkin, Johor Bahru
  3. How to order: walk in, WhatsApp, or chaleurpatisserie.orderla.my
  4. #chaleuratelierpatisserie plus a few of: #JBDesserts #JohorBahru #Larkin #JBFoodie #DessertJB #Entremets

Questions

Our collabs are a dessert experience in exchange for a Reel. If you work on paid rates, send your rate card to our team and we'll come back to you.

Can I bring a friend or get more items?

Ask our team in WhatsApp. We'll check and come back to you.

Can I do a Story instead of a Reel?

Our collab is one Reel, as it stays on your profile. Ask us if you have another idea.

Can I choose my date?

Put your preferred date in the form. We confirm it once your collab is approved.

Is it halal?

No alcohol, no pork, no lard.

Can I earn commission if people order?

Yes, through our separate Partner Programme (12% on orders through your own link). See the Partner Guide.
