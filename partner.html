What you earn

Commission
On every paid order through your own link, from your very first order. No cap, no targets. Your rate is confirmed to you privately when you're approved.

What counts as a sale

Counts
  • Orders placed through your personal link
  • Paid online through Chaleur's checkout
  • The product price after any discount
Doesn't count
  • Delivery fees
  • Unpaid, cancelled or refunded orders
  • Orders not placed through your link
  • Duplicate, test or fraudulent orders

If an order is refunded after you've been paid, the commission on it is taken off your next statement.

How you get paid

  1. Once approved: we send you a welcome message on WhatsApp with your personal link, your Orderla login, your private payout-details form and the invite to the Chaleur Partners WhatsApp group.
  2. Payout details: fill in the private form from your welcome message: your name as on your bank account, bank, account number and the last 4 digits of your IC. You can start sharing straight away. Commission is kept for you until these details are verified.
  3. Any time: log in at orderla.my/login-sp to see the orders placed through your link.
  4. 1st to 7th of the month: we check last month's orders and send your statement on WhatsApp: paid orders, eligible sales and commission.
  5. By the 14th: we transfer your commission to your bank and message you when it's sent. Small balances may roll over to the next month. Example: October sales are paid by 14 November.
Your payout details are safe. We only ask for bank details after you're approved, through a private form that only management can see. We never ask for passwords, PINs or OTPs.

Product credit for creators

Selected active creators may be offered Chaleur product credit to feature in their content. It's at Chaleur's discretion.

  1. Post within 7 days. Publish original content featuring the product and send us the link.
  2. Say it was gifted. For example: "Product credit provided by Chaleur Atelier."
  3. Product credit, not cash. It doesn't carry over, can't be exchanged and earns no commission.
  4. Keep your post up. Missing or deleted content may stop future credit.

Tips that sell

  1. Show the cake being cut. A short video of the first slice beats a photo of the box.
  2. Post before occasions. Birthdays, Mother's Day, Hari Raya, Deepavali, Christmas, year-end parties. Share your link a week before.
  3. Put your link everywhere. Instagram bio, TikTok bio, WhatsApp status, the end of every message to a friend.
  4. Ask customers to order through your link. Orders placed any other way can't be credited to you.

Programme rules

  • Use Chaleur's real prices and product details. Don't promise discounts or delivery times we haven't announced.
  • Never collect money from customers yourself. Customers always pay through Chaleur's online checkout.
  • Only orders placed through your link can be credited. Orders by WhatsApp, phone or at the shop can't be traced to you.
  • If a post was supported by Chaleur (for example a cake we gave you), say so clearly.
  • Say "No alcohol, no pork, no lard". Please don't say "halal".
  • No spam, fake reviews or misleading claims. We may pause a link while we look into a problem.

Questions

A customer ordered but I can't see it

The customer needs to order through your link. Check your Orderla login first. If it's still missing, send Affiliate Support the order number and the customer's name.

When is my first payment?

Sales are counted by calendar month and paid by the 14th of the following month. November sales are paid by 14 December. Your payout details need to be verified first.

Can I lose my commission?

Commission you've earned stays yours. Only refunded, cancelled or ineligible orders are taken off, and you'll see any change on your statement.

A customer has a problem with their order

Send them to Chaleur Customer Support on +60 14-665 0928. We handle orders, delivery and after-sales.

Can my friends join too?

Yes. Send them this guide; the Apply button is at the top.

Can I buy for myself through my link?

Orders arranged mainly to earn commission don't count. If you'd like product for content, ask us about product credit.
