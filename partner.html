How you get paid

  1. Once approved: we send you a welcome message on WhatsApp with your personal link, your Orderla login, your private payout-details form and the invite to the Chaleur Partners WhatsApp group.
  2. Payout details: fill in the private form from your welcome message: your name as on your bank account, bank, account number and the last 4 digits of your IC. You can start sharing straight away. Commission is kept for you until these details are verified.
  3. Any time: log in at orderla.my/login-sp to see the orders placed through your link.
  4. 1st to 7th of the month: we check last month's orders and send your statement on WhatsApp: paid orders, eligible sales and commission.
  5. By the 14th: we transfer your commission to your bank and message you when it's sent. Balances under RM10 roll into the next month. Example: October sales are paid by 14 November.
Your payout details are safe. We only ask for bank details after you're approved, through a private form that only management can see. We never ask for passwords, PINs or OTPs.

RM50 monthly creator credit

Selected creators can get RM50 of Chaleur products each month to feature in their content. It's given on approval only.

  1. One credit per month. Choose an order of RM50 or more; pay any amount above RM50 and the delivery fee yourself.
  2. Post within 7 days. Publish original content featuring the product and send us the link.
  3. Say it was gifted. For example: "Product credit provided by Chaleur Atelier."
  4. Product credit, not cash. It doesn't carry over and can't be exchanged. The credit order earns no commission.
  5. For your content only. Can't be combined with other promotions or used for a customer's order.
  6. Keep your post up. Missing or deleted content may stop future credits.

Tips that sell

  1. Show the cake being cut. A short video of the first slice beats a photo of the box.
  2. Post before occasions. Birthdays, Mother's Day, Hari Raya, Deepavali, Christmas, year-end parties. Share your link a week before.
  3. Put your link everywhere. Instagram bio, TikTok bio, WhatsApp status, the end of every message to a friend.
  4. Ask customers to order through your link. Orders placed any other way can't be credited to you.

Programme rules

  • Use Chaleur's real prices and product details. Don't promise discounts or delivery times we haven't announced.
  • Never collect money from customers yourself. Customers always pay through Chaleur's online checkout.
  • Only orders placed through your link can be credited. Orders by WhatsApp, phone or at the shop can't be traced to you.
  • If a post was supported by Chaleur (for example a cake we gave you), say so clearly.
  • Say "No alcohol, no pork, no lard". Please don't say "halal".
  • No spam, fake reviews or misleading claims. We may pause a link while we look into a problem.
  • This is a freelance, commission-only arrangement. It isn't employment; no fixed hours or targets.

Key terms

By applying and using your partner link, you agree to these terms. Programme v2, effective 1 November 2026.

  1. Commission. 12% of eligible sales: the paid product value of orders placed through your assigned Orderla link, after discounts. Delivery fees are excluded. Cancelled, refunded, charged-back, unpaid, duplicate, test, fraudulent or wrongly attributed orders don't earn commission, and neither do orders arranged mainly to generate commission.
  2. Records and payment. Chaleur's reconciled Orderla and payment records decide eligible sales and commission. Commission is paid by the 14th of the following month to a bank account in your own name, once your payout details are verified. Until then it's held for you. Balances under RM10 carry forward. Commission on orders refunded or cancelled after payment is deducted from your next statement.
  3. Your link and your content. Use only your assigned link and Chaleur's real products, prices and campaign details. Content must be accurate and say clearly when a product was gifted. If we ask you to correct or remove harmful, misleading or unauthorised content, please do so promptly. Ignoring a removal request may lead to suspension, removal from the programme and loss of commission on sales from that content.
  4. Orders and payments. Customers always pay Chaleur Atelier F&B Sdn Bhd through the official online checkout. Never collect customer money and never change an order's status or payment status in Orderla.
  5. Changes to the programme. Chaleur may change, pause or end the programme, the commission rate, these rules or the creator credit. Changes are announced in the partner WhatsApp group with the date they take effect. Commission already approved for payment won't be reduced, except for orders later found ineligible, refunded, cancelled, fraudulent or charged back.
  6. Not employment. Voluntary, non-exclusive, commission-only. No guaranteed selection, salary, benefits, minimum income or creator credit. You're responsible for any tax on your own earnings.
  7. Ending your participation. You or Chaleur can end it at any time by letting the other know. Eligible commission earned before the end date is paid in the normal monthly cycle.
  8. Age and privacy. You must be 18 or older. We use your application and payout details only to assess your application, contact you, and run and pay the programme.

Questions

A customer ordered but I can't see it

The customer needs to order through your link. Check your Orderla login first. If it's still missing, send Affiliate Support the order number and the customer's name.

When is my first payment?

Sales are counted by calendar month and paid by the 14th of the following month. November sales are paid by 14 December. Your payout details need to be verified first.

Can I lose my commission?

Commission you've earned stays yours. Only refunded, cancelled or ineligible orders are taken off, and you'll see any change on your statement.

A customer has a problem with their order

Send them to Chaleur Customer Support on +60 14-665 0928. We handle orders, delivery and after-sales.

Can my friends join too?

Yes. Send them this guide; the Apply button is at the top.

Can I buy for myself through my link?

Orders arranged mainly to earn commission don't count. If you'd like product for content, ask about the creator credit.
